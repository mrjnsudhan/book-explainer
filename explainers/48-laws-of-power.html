A law-by-law illustrated guide to Robert Greene's book on power

The 48 Laws of Power,
Explained

Forty-eight rules drawn from three thousand years of courts, wars and cons. Each law here gets its own illustration, the idea in plain language, the history Greene uses to prove it, and the reversal, when breaking the law works better. After every eight laws there's a workshop with a hands-on exercise and a quick quiz. Mark a law understood and your progress is remembered on this device.

Read it as a moveHow people have gained, kept and lost power, and why the same patterns keep repeating.
Read it as a warningEvery law is also a move someone may be making on you. Each one ends with how to spot it and what to do.

An independent study guide to The 48 Laws of Power by Robert Greene (1998). The explanations and illustrations are original, written and drawn in our own words; the historical stories are retold briefly from the examples the book uses, and the exercises are simplified models of its ideas. The book describes how power has been played, not how anyone should treat people, so this guide gives every law a defence as well as a move.
